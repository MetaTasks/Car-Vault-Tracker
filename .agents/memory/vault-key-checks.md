---
name: Vault key checks
description: The user's workflow expectation for checking key location during plate scans.
---

When staff scan a vehicle registration, ask whether that vehicle's keys have been checked and are secured in the locked vault. Most keys are kept there; the app should also represent keys that are not in the vault or have not been checked.

**Why:** The user said most vehicle keys are inside a locked vault and wants the scan flow to include a key-inventory question.

**How to apply:** Keep key confirmation separate from marking the vehicle on site. Never infer that keys are in the vault merely because the car is present.
